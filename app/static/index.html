Pano -> SHARP -> World PLY

Pano Sharp Studio

Generate a seamless equirectangular panorama with GPT Image 2, fracture it into perspective views that Apple SHARP can understand, and splice the resulting Gaussian splat PLY files into one approximate world.

This app is meant for local research and prototyping. The setup doctor on the right checks whether `uv`, SHARP, Torch, and your OpenAI key are lined up before you spend time waiting on a run that was never going to work.
